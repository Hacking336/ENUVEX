How this works

  • Local demand (50%) — how many active postings in your area require the skill.
  • Opportunity impact (30%) — how many jobs reach the match line with that skill.
  • Preference relevance (20%) — how many of those postings fit your municipality or preferred employment type.

If a factor cannot be calculated from stored data, it is left out and the remaining weights are renormalized. Priority is a recommendation ranking, not a guarantee of employment.
